Handling of Photos (Key Points)

  • Registered photos of people, clothing images and generated results are stored only on your device.
  • Images are sent only when you execute a generation, via our server (Google Cloud Platform), to generative AI (the OpenAI API provided by OpenAI, or, as a backup, the Gemini API provided by Google LLC). Images are sent to OpenAI only if, in addition, you have given consent on a confirmation screen shown once in the App before first use (see Section 3 for details of the destinations).
  • Images sent are processed in memory on our server and then discarded immediately; they are not stored. They are not recorded in our database either.
  • When you execute a generation, you are treated as having consented to this sending (generation is not possible without sending).
  • We do not obtain your name or email address.
  • We do not engage in any ad delivery or behavioral tracking.

* Because images are sent to Google or OpenAI at the time of generation, we do not claim that “images never leave your device.” We describe the actual behavior accurately.

3. Handling of Image Data

Because it relates to the core function of the App, we explain the handling of images separately.

3-1. Storage on Your Device

The photos of people you register, the clothing images you import, the generated try-on images and Fit Report images are stored only on your device, in an area dedicated to the App, and are not stored on our server. They cannot be accessed by other apps and are protected so that they cannot be read while the device is locked. They are also excluded from iCloud backup.

3-2. Sending to Our Server and to the Gemini API

Only when you execute a try-on generation are your photos of people and clothing images sent to our server (Google Cloud Platform, region in Japan). For Standard quality in version 2.1 and earlier of the App, and in version 2.2 and later when the OpenAI API is temporarily unavailable, they are sent from there to “Gemini API”, a generative AI service provided by Google LLC (for other destinations, see Section 3-2-2). Executing a generation presupposes consent to this sending. Generation cannot be performed without sending.

Google LLC handles the data as follows.

  • We use the paid API, and under Google’s terms, the images sent are not used to train Google’s AI models.
  • However, Google may temporarily retain data sent to the API for the period specified in its policies, for purposes such as abuse monitoring.
  • Processing by Google may take place on servers outside Japan (such as in the United States).
  • For details, please see the Gemini API Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3-2-2. Sending to the OpenAI API

In the following cases, your photos of people and clothing images are sent via our server to the “OpenAI API” provided by OpenAI (the image generation model GPT Image and the image-reading model GPT). Images are sent to the OpenAI API only if, in addition to the consent given by executing a generation (Section 3-2), you have reviewed the destination and given consent on a confirmation screen shown once in the App before first use.

  • When generating try-on images in version 2.2 and later of the App (all generations)
  • When generating with “High Quality” selected in version 2.1 of the App
  • When creating a Fit Report (似合う診断レポート; version 2.2 and later). For the diagnosis, in addition to Registered Photos, try-on images and images of the clothing tried on, images of clothing saved in your wardrobe (up to 12 items) are also sent

In version 2.2 and later, when the OpenAI API is temporarily unavailable, generation may be performed as a backup using the Gemini API described in Section 3-2.

  • Under OpenAI’s terms, data sent to the API is not used to train OpenAI’s models.
  • However, OpenAI may retain data sent to the API for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Processing by OpenAI takes place on servers outside Japan (such as in the United States).
  • For details, please see the OpenAI Services Agreement and the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

3-3. Disposal of Images Sent

Our server only processes the images it receives in memory and does not write them to disk or to any storage service. Images are discarded immediately after processing is completed. Only the metadata described in Section 2 is recorded in our database. Neither image data nor any part of it is output to logs.

3-4. How to Delete

Images on your device are deleted when you retake or delete photos in the App, or when you uninstall the App. Because no images are stored on our server, there is no need to request deletion of images on the server side.

3-5. Body Information and Garment Measurements (Size Check, Optional)

  1. If you use the size check (version 2.4 or later), the height, weight, usual clothing size and body measurements you enter are stored only on your device (Keychain) and are not synchronized to other devices. Garment sizes and measurements are stored on your device together with try-on results and your clothes.
  2. They are sent to our server, together with the images, only when you run a generation or Fit Report that uses the size check. Our server uses them only to calculate the size check, neither stores them nor writes them to logs, and discards them as soon as processing is complete.
  3. To the contractors that generate images and diagnoses (Sections 3-2 and 3-2-2), we send only the visual description calculated on our server (for example, "the hem falls around the knee") and the height and size labels, as text. Your weight and body measurements themselves are not sent to these contractors.
  4. You can delete the body information you entered at any time from My Studio in the App. It is also deleted when you delete your account. All other features of the Service are available without entering it.

3-6. Closet Item Details and Reading Shared Product Pages

  1. The details you record for items in your Closet (name, brand, price, store, product page URL, size, color, material, season, purchase date, notes and status; version 2.4 or later; entering them is optional) are stored only on your device and are not sent to our server.
  2. When you share a page, such as an online store's product page, to the App, the product information published on that page (product name, brand, price, currency, site name, color and material) is read on your device and filled into the details automatically. To fill in items that could not be read, the App may access the shared page directly from your device. Neither the content nor the URL of the page is sent to our server.
  3. You can also choose not to save a shared item to your Closet. In that case, the item's image and details are deleted from your device after the try-on. Items and details you save can be deleted from your Closet at any time.

6. Retention Period

  • Image data: Not stored on our server (Section 3). Data on your device is retained until you delete it.
  • Generation request records: Automatically deleted once 30 days have passed from the date of recording.
  • Account information, credit information and purchase information: Retained while the account is active and for any period required by law. If you request deletion, we delete it through the procedure in Section 8.

7. Security Control Measures

  • All communications are encrypted using HTTPS.
  • API keys for generative AI are not included in the App itself and are managed as secrets on the server side.
  • Reading from and writing to the database is restricted so that it can be performed only from our server.
  • The operating system’s data protection features are applied to images on your device so that they cannot be read while the device is locked.
  • Our operations do not output image data or authentication tokens to logs.
